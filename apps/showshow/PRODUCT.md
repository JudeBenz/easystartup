# PRODUCT.md

## Users
Exhibiting artists (primary), showgoers, and verified show directors. Many artists are 50–80; interfaces must stay readable outdoors and on phones.

## Mode
Operate. Design serves the task: find a show, log ROI, track an application. Marketing flourish stays secondary.

## Brand voice
Plain, specific, unhurried. Prefer “Booth fee $875” over “unlock insights.”

## Anti-references
Purple gradients. Glassmorphism. Cream/beige “tasteful AI” surfaces. Hero metric strips. Eyebrow kickers. Pill chip nav forests. Pulsing lines. Inter/Geist/Instrument Serif defaults.
